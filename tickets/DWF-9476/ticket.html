Known DWLite behaviour (Jaival Wadhvana)

DWLite has a known issue when multiple surveys/tasks are opened in the same browser. DWLite can only sync one task at a time in IndexedDB; conflicts can cause inconsistent behaviour.

General root cause

After analysing all 5 tasks and DEV testing, root cause is likely browser/session caching behaviour (DWLite IndexedDB). Exact prod corruption was not reproduced on DEV.

  • Multiple Ministry approval URLs opened/executed simultaneously in same browser → route may not complete
  • Prod patterns: (a) route stopped at IO approval with no Ministry logs, or (b) partial route → incomplete doc data
  • DEV testing: "Form Active in Another Tab" and "Invalid survey page" — but not exact prod corruption
  • Fresh browser session + one URL at a time → Ministry route completed successfully in testing
Conclusion — investigation done; recovery pending
  • Root cause identified: likely DWLite multi-tab / IndexedDB conflict — not a Lua workflow/config defect
  • None of the 5 tasks have been corrected yet — they remain manually completed in prod
  • Recovery steps documented per task above; awaiting client to execute
  • Client guidance: Incognito/Private window; open and execute only one Ministry approval URL at a time
